FOUNDRYLAB SOLUTIONS — EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK
Training document for Microsoft Foundry practice only.
This is fictional data and does not represent any real company.

1. OFFICE SCHEDULE
Standard working hours are 9:15 AM to 6:15 PM, Monday through Friday.

2. HYBRID WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely every Wednesday and Friday.
New employees become eligible for hybrid work after completing 14 calendar days.

3. EQUIPMENT REQUESTS
Laptop, monitor, and accessory requests must be submitted through the internal IT request system.
Requests above PHP 25,000 require approval from the Operations Manager.

4. ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time employees receive 17 paid annual leave days per calendar year.
Leave requests of three consecutive working days or more should be submitted at least 10 calendar days in advance.

5. SECURITY
Employees must report a lost company device to the IT Security Desk within 30 minutes of discovering the loss.
Company passwords must not be shared through email, chat, or personal note-taking applications.

6. AI USAGE POLICY
Employees may use approved generative AI tools for drafting, research, coding assistance, and summarization.
Confidential client data must not be submitted to unapproved public AI services.
AI-generated outputs used in customer deliverables must be reviewed by a human employee before release.

7. SUPPORT ESCALATION
Priority-1 customer incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and escalated to the on-call Solutions Engineer.

8. UNIQUE VERIFICATION FACT
The internal code phrase for this training handbook is ORBIT-MANGO-742.
This code exists only so the learner can verify that the AI agent retrieved information from this document.
